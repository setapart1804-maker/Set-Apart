COOKIE POLICY

This Cookie Policy explains how SET APART currently uses browser storage, cookies, and similar technologies on our website.

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small files that websites may store on a user's device to remember information, support website functions, measure usage, or provide personalized experiences.

Websites may also use similar technologies such as local storage and session storage.

2. HOW SET APART CURRENTLY USES STORAGE

SET APART currently uses browser storage primarily to support essential shopping functionality.

For example, browser storage may be used to:

  • Remember products added to your cart
  • Store selected product options such as color, size, or quantity
  • Temporarily maintain checkout-related information during the ordering process
  • Support normal website functionality

3. ESSENTIAL STORAGE

Some browser storage is necessary for features such as the shopping cart and checkout experience to function correctly.

These technologies are used for essential operational purposes rather than advertising or behavioral profiling.

4. ANALYTICS COOKIES

At the time this policy was last updated, SET APART does not intentionally use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar analytics or advertising tracking tools on the website.

Because those tools are not currently enabled by SET APART, we do not currently use analytics cookies for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Some third-party services used by SET APART may set or use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies independently.

PAYPAL

SET APART uses PayPal for payment processing. PayPal may use its own cookies and technologies when its payment services are loaded or used.

These technologies are controlled by PayPal and are subject to PayPal's own privacy and cookie policies.

HOSTING AND TECHNICAL SERVICES

Hosting, server, or security providers may process technical information that is necessary to deliver, secure, or operate the website.

6. MARKETING AND ADVERTISING COOKIES

SET APART does not currently intentionally use advertising pixels or marketing cookies for personalized advertising.

If advertising or marketing tracking is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated and appropriate consent tools will be implemented where required.

7. COOKIE CONSENT

Because SET APART currently relies mainly on essential website functionality and does not intentionally use non-essential analytics or advertising tracking tools, no non-essential analytics tracking is currently activated by SET APART.

If non-essential tracking technologies are added in the future, users may be shown a cookie consent banner or preference control before those technologies are activated where required by applicable law.

8. MANAGING COOKIES AND STORAGE

Most browsers allow users to view, block, or delete cookies and browser storage through their browser settings.

Blocking essential storage may affect certain website features, including the ability to maintain products in your shopping cart.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

SET APART may update this Cookie Policy when new technology, analytics tools, marketing services, or third-party integrations are introduced.

The latest version will be published on this page.

10. CONTACT

If you have questions about cookies, browser storage, or privacy practices, contact SET APART through our contact page.
