WALK BY FAITH

PILGRIM represents a journey of faith. Its message is a reminder that living with purpose does not mean knowing every step in advance. For SET APART, faith-inspired streetwear is about identity and conviction beyond a single outfit: choose what you believe and keep moving forward with intention.

The PILGRIM design appears on an oversized hoodie and a T-shirt. These are two ways to wear the same walk-by-faith message. Each piece is explored and purchased through the official SET APART collection, where you can check current sizes, colors, prices and stock before ordering.
