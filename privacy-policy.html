PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how SET APART collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you visit our website, place an order, or contact us.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate our website, process orders, provide customer service, and deliver products.

ORDER AND CHECKOUT INFORMATION

When you place an order, we may collect information such as:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Shipping address
  • Apartment or address details, when provided
  • City, state or region, country, and postal code
  • Phone number
  • Products ordered, size, color, quantity, and order details

This information is used to process, confirm, prepare, and ship your order.

PAYMENT INFORMATION

Payments are processed through PayPal. SET APART does not directly collect or store your full credit card or debit card details on this website.

PayPal may collect and process payment information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

CONTACT INFORMATION

If you contact SET APART through a contact form or another communication method, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and message.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To process and manage your orders
  • To confirm successful payments
  • To prepare and ship products
  • To send order confirmation and shipping-related communications
  • To respond to customer service requests
  • To prevent misuse, fraud, or security issues
  • To maintain and improve the operation of the website
  • To comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations

3. SHOPPING CART AND BROWSER STORAGE

SET APART may use browser storage, including local storage or session storage, to support essential website functions such as remembering items in your cart and temporarily maintaining checkout information.

This storage is used for website functionality and is not currently used by SET APART for advertising profiles or cross-site behavioral tracking.

4. COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

At the time this Privacy Policy was last updated, SET APART does not intentionally use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar advertising analytics tools on the website.

Third-party services used by the website, including payment providers, may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

If SET APART introduces non-essential analytics, advertising, or behavioral tracking technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy and our cookie practices will be updated as appropriate.

5. MARKETING COMMUNICATIONS

Placing an order does not automatically enroll you in marketing communications.

If SET APART introduces newsletters, promotional emails, SMS marketing, or similar services, users will be provided with an appropriate way to give consent where required and to unsubscribe from future marketing communications.

6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

SET APART uses third-party service providers when necessary to operate the store.

PAYPAL

PayPal processes payments and may receive information necessary to complete a transaction.

EMAIL SERVICES

SET APART may use an email delivery provider to send transactional messages such as order confirmations and shipping notifications.

HOSTING AND SERVER SERVICES

Our website and backend services may be hosted or operated using third-party infrastructure providers. These providers may process limited technical information necessary to deliver and secure the service.

Third-party providers are governed by their own privacy practices and legal terms.

7. DATA SHARING

SET APART does not sell your personal information.

We may share information only when reasonably necessary with service providers involved in:

  • Payment processing
  • Order confirmation
  • Shipping and delivery
  • Website hosting and technical operation
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Compliance with applicable law

8. DATA RETENTION

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including order fulfillment, customer support, accounting, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

Temporary information stored in your browser may remain until it is deleted, expires, or is cleared through your browser settings.

9. DATA SECURITY

SET APART takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

SET APART is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe personal information from a child has been submitted improperly, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12. INTERNATIONAL USERS

Customers may access SET APART from different countries. Information may therefore be processed through service providers located in jurisdictions other than the customer's own.

Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international data processing and transfers.

13. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

SET APART may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, business practices, or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be made available on this page with an updated effective date.

14. CONTACT

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, please contact SET APART through our contact page.
