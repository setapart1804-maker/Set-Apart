Order Processing

Orders are processed after payment has been successfully confirmed.

Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information.

  • Sector or delivery address
  • Any additional information required for delivery

Incorrect or incomplete information may delay delivery.

Questions About Your Order

If you have questions regarding your shipment or delivery, please contact setapart-ht through the contact information provided on our website.
