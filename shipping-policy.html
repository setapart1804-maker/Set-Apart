SET APART POLICIES

SHIPPING POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026

SET APART currently ships within the Dominican Republic.

Shipping Rates

Shipping fees are calculated based on the delivery destination.

Santo Domingo area: RD$300

Other cities in the Dominican Republic: RD$500

The applicable shipping fee will be displayed during checkout before payment is completed.

Order Processing

Orders are processed after payment has been successfully confirmed.

Once your order is prepared and handed over for delivery, its shipping status may be updated in our system.

Tracking

When a tracking number is provided by the delivery carrier, SET APART may provide that tracking information to the customer.

Tracking availability may depend on the carrier used for the shipment.

Delivery Times

Delivery times may vary depending on destination, carrier operations, weekends, holidays, weather conditions, or circumstances outside of SET APART's control.

SET APART does not guarantee an exact delivery date unless specifically communicated to the customer.

Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information.

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • City
  • Sector or delivery address
  • Any additional information required for delivery

Incorrect or incomplete information may delay delivery.

Questions About Your Order

If you have questions regarding your shipment or delivery, please contact SET APART through the contact information provided on our website.
