SALES POLICY

This Sales Policy explains the basic terms that apply when purchasing products from SET APART.

1. PRODUCT INFORMATION

SET APART makes reasonable efforts to provide accurate product descriptions, images, sizes, colors, and prices.

Colors may appear slightly different depending on lighting, photography, screen settings, or device display.

Product availability may change at any time.

2. PRICES

Product prices are displayed on the website in the currency shown at checkout.

SET APART may change prices at any time before an order is completed.

Once payment has been successfully completed, the price confirmed for that order will apply, subject to any correction required for an obvious technical or pricing error.

3. ORDERS

Adding a product to your cart does not reserve the product.

An order is considered submitted when the customer completes the checkout process and payment is successfully authorized or captured.

SET APART may contact the customer if additional information is required to process or fulfill an order.

4. PAYMENT

Payments are currently processed using PayPal and any payment options made available through PayPal.

Payment authorization, approval, and processing may be subject to PayPal's terms, fraud checks, availability, and payment-source requirements.

SET APART does not guarantee that every payment attempt will be approved.

After a successful order, SET APART may send an order confirmation to the email address provided at checkout.

Customers are responsible for providing a valid and accurate email address.

Customers should review their order details and contact SET APART promptly if they notice an error.

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address.

SET APART may not be responsible for delays, failed deliveries, or additional costs caused by incorrect or incomplete information provided by the customer.

Shipping methods, costs, and delivery estimates may vary depending on the destination and shipping provider.

7. SHIPPING CONFIRMATION

When an order is shipped, SET APART may send a shipping confirmation containing the carrier and tracking number, when available.

Tracking information is provided by the shipping carrier and may take time to become active after the package is handed over.

8. DELIVERY TIMES

Any delivery timeframe shown or communicated is an estimate unless otherwise expressly stated.

Delivery may be affected by customs, weather, carrier delays, local conditions, holidays, or other circumstances outside SET APART's reasonable control.

Customers are responsible for checking any import requirements that may apply in their destination country.

If you need to request a cancellation, contact SET APART as soon as possible.

We cannot guarantee that an order can be cancelled after payment has been completed, especially once preparation or shipping has started.

Returns, exchanges, and refunds are governed by the separate SET APART Refund Policy.

If you receive an item that appears damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact SET APART as soon as reasonably possible.
