PUT GOD FIRST

GOD FIRST expresses a priority, not just a phrase on a garment. The design carries a reminder to place faith at the center of everyday decisions and to keep your values present in what you do. SET APART uses streetwear to express that message without separating it from the way we live.

The GOD FIRST collection includes an oversized hoodie and a T-shirt for different everyday looks.
