About SET APART

SET APART is more than a clothing brand. It is a movement that encourages believers to live boldly for Christ and reflect His light in everyday life.

Every hoodie and every design carries a message of faith, hope, and purpose. Our goal is to create premium streetwear that starts conversations about Jesus.

Our Mission:
To inspire people to live differently, stand firm in their faith, and represent Christ with confidence.
